# Phase 1C-3 Extreme Points

漠河、抚远、喀什 are adapted from the audited `border` control points to the
presentation role `extreme-point`. Their original V coordinates and WGS84
coordinates are unchanged; they are clickable and can participate in a
comparison. 曾母暗沙 remains a south-sea geographic landmark and is excluded
from the mainland city marker layer and shortcut list.
